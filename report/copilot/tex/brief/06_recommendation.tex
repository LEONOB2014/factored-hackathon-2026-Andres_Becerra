\section{Recommended configuration and limits}

Run the copilot with \model{Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507} on Nebius Token Factory. It was as safe as every model inside
BETA AID and as accurate as the evaluations can tell (\est{mod.qwen30-tf-correct-compared}{num} of
\est{mod.qwen30-tf-correct-compared}{den} compared cases), and it was the fastest and steadiest hosted option: a
slowest call of \est{scr.qwen30-tf-max}{value}\,ms against a timeout of \est{sys.model-timeout}{value}\,s, and a valid
label on \est{scr.qwen30-tf-valid}{num} of \est{scr.qwen30-tf-valid}{den} calls. The copilot remains fully usable
without a model; the model adds the informal phrasings the copilot would otherwise ask the customer to rephrase.

The limits are those of the samples. The frozen set cannot rank models that differ by a few cases; each model ran once
at temperature zero; the attacks are public prompts adapted to the card service; the conversations use demonstration
customers; and the retrieval threshold rests on a handful of unanswerable questions. The runs recorded no prices, so
cost per case is not reported; the evaluation report gives the tokens per call for pricing at any provider's rates.
