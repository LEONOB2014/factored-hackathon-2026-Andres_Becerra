\section{What the copilot does}

BETA AID is the customer-service copilot of a demonstration bank in Mexico, Colombia and Argentina. It serves card
customers in Spanish and Portuguese: it answers questions about a card's status, balance and limit, declined
payments and expiry; it blocks, unblocks and reissues cards after the customer confirms; and it hands fraud,
disputes, complaints and limit increases to a person with a prepared packet, so the customer does not repeat
themselves. A customer signs in first, and the copilot only ever reads that customer's own cards.

The copilot is built so that no language model decides what happens to a customer. A trained intent model, a
versioned policy and verified tools decide; templates state the facts the tools return. A language model can be
plugged in to help with wording and with messages the intent model is unsure of, and the copilot works, with the same
safety, without one. This brief summarises the system and its evaluation; the evaluation report gives every number
with its interval and source.
