\section{What the evaluations show}

Proportions below carry Wilson intervals at confidence level \proto{stats.level} \parencite{wilson1927}; paired comparisons use the exact
McNemar test \parencite{mcnemar1947}, adjusted for false discoveries within each family \parencite{benjamini1995}.

\paragraph{The copilot understands customers better than keywords.} On \est{int.held-out}{value} phrasings written
before tuning, the intent model is right on \est{int.acc-learned}{num} (\est{int.acc-learned}{pct}\,\%, interval
\est{int.acc-learned}{pct_ci}\,\%), keywords on \est{int.acc-keyword}{num} ($p = \est{int.mcnemar}{p}$). Above its
threshold it labels \est{int.cover}{pct}\,\% of phrasings on its own and is right on \est{int.acc-confident}{pct}\,\%
of them. On whole conversations, the copilot without any language model ends \est{chl.first-learned-correct}{num} of
\est{chl.cases}{value} frozen cases correctly, against \est{chl.first-keyword-correct}{num} with keyword routing
($p = \est{chl.mcnemar}{p}$), and misses \est{chl.first-learned-missed}{num} of the
\est{chl.first-learned-missed}{den} cases a person must decide.

\paragraph{It cannot be talked into breaking the rules, whatever the model.} Alone, the models broke the card-service
rules on as many as \est{atk.nemotron4b-raw}{num} of \est{atk.attacks}{value} attacks, mostly by repeating a full card
number or reciting their instructions. Inside BETA AID none did, for any model (\cref{fig:brief-violations}); with no
violation in \est{atk.attacks}{value} attacks each rate is below \est{atk.qwen30-tf-wrapped-upper}{value} with
confidence \proto{stats.level}. The same \est{atk.refused-by-all}{value} attacks were refused under every model: the
safety is the copilot's, not the model's.

\paragraph{The models do not differ in accuracy, but they do in speed.} Inside BETA AID the models ended within a few
cases of the copilot without a model on the \est{mod.compared-cases}{value} cases they could be compared on
(\cref{fig:brief-forest}); none differs detectably ($q \le \proto{stats.alpha}$ for \est{mod.vs-base-q05}{value} of
\est{atk.runs}{value}). Every model rescued the same \est{mod.common-gains}{value} informal phrasings, and lost a few
multi-card follow-ups or an out-of-scope request. In speed they separate clearly (\cref{fig:brief-speed}):
\model{Qwen3-30B-A3B} on Token Factory answers the labelling job in a median of \est{scr.qwen30-tf-p50}{estimate}\,ms,
\est{scr.qwen30-fl-minus-tf}{estimate}\,ms faster than on Featherless; \model{Qwen2.5-14B} exceeded the copilot's
timeout on \est{scr.qwen14-fl-over}{value} of \est{scr.qwen14-fl-ok}{value} calls, and \model{DeepSeek-V4-Flash}
returned a usable label on at most \est{scr.dsv4flash-tf-valid}{num} of \est{scr.dsv4flash-tf-valid}{den}.

\paragraph{Governed retrieval keeps unapproved documents out.} No retriever returned a draft, superseded, retired or
expired document. The served retriever ranks the right document first for \est{kb.bundled-hit1}{num} of
\est{kb.bundled-hit1}{den} answerable questions, but its threshold rejected none of the
\est{kb.bundled-rejected}{den} unanswerable ones; the grounding check and the hand-off to a person keep that from
becoming an unverified answer.

\paragraph{A defect found and corrected.} The stored model runs lacked the retrieval package, so every knowledge case
failed in them for that reason alone. Replayed with retrieval working, the hosted models resolve
\est{mod.qwen30-tf-replay-passed}{value} of the \est{mod.knowledge-cases}{value} knowledge cases, preferring a hand-off
to an answer that fails the grounding check. The evaluation harnesses now stop when retrieval cannot run.
