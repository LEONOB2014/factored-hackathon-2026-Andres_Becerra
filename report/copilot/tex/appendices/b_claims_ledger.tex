\chapter{Claims ledger}\label{app:claims-ledger}

Every number the chapters cite is a field of a row of this ledger. A row records the claim's kind, its value and
interval, the method of the interval, and the stored result it was computed from, numbered in the table of sources at
the end, which gives the result's path and the first characters of its digest. A claim without an interval says why:
\emph{constant}, a threshold or parameter read from its source; \emph{exact count}, a number of items or events; or
the reason a stored figure carries no interval. All claims are exploratory (E): the report's analysis was not
registered before the evaluations ran.
