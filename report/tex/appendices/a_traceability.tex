\chapter{Traceability matrix}\label{app:traceability}

The report sets out to cover every method of the notebooks behind the Grain Atlas, and this appendix is the evidence
that it does. The notebook map lists every heading of the first and second level in the $\est{ch01.ledger-notebooks}{value}$
notebook sources, parsed from their jupytext files, and names for each heading the chapter that treats it or the reason
it is out of scope. The test suite parses the sources again on every build and fails if a heading is missing from the
map or has neither a chapter nor a reason. Of the $\est{ch01.ledger-headings}{value}$ headings,
$\est{ch01.ledger-headings-mapped}{value}$ are treated in a chapter and $\est{ch01.ledger-headings-out}{value}$ are
interactive widgets, whose content is covered by the sections they explore.

Notebooks run once per market (the country template's \ident{country\_all}, \ident{country\_ar},
\ident{country\_co} and \ident{country\_mx} runs, and the backup runs) share one source and appear once.
\Cref{tab:app-traceability} groups the headings by notebook; an indented heading is a section of the heading above it.
