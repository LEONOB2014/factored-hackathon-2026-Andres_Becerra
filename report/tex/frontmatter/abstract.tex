\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

BETA AID's data platform decides which machine-learning products LATAM Bank's data can support through readiness
gates, built on a series of exploratory notebooks published as the Grain Atlas. This report examines the mathematical
and statistical foundations of every method in those notebooks, from key and set comparisons, clock detection and
record linkage, through anomaly detection, change points, conformance, privacy and audit, to aggregation, survival,
forecasting, queueing and learnability. For each method it states the assumptions, checks them on the data, and
re-estimates the uncertainty that the notebooks left out, with intervals over the independent unit, false-discovery
control over families declared in advance, and a registered re-estimation of every gate under a protocol frozen before
it ran. Every number in the text is generated from a ledger of $\est{ch01.ledger-claims}{value}$ claims, each tied by
its digest to the artefact it was computed from, and all $\est{ch01.ledger-headings}{value}$ headings of the
$\est{ch01.ledger-notebooks}{value}$ notebook sources are traced to the chapter that treats them.

The data are sound in form and nearly empty of learnable behaviour. The second copy that arrived with the dataset is an
incomplete, independently drawn replicate of the same fixed-parameter process with targeted defects, not a copy of the
main data, and it serves as one throughout. Of $\est{ch18.cells}{value}$ evaluations of candidate targets by market and copy,
$\est{ch18.no-signal}{value}$ show no evidence of signal; fraud is predictable only through a score that encodes the
label, and the one learnable target, dormancy, is ranked as well as by boosting, and calibrated, by a gamma-Poisson
model with a pair of parameters. The registered re-estimation leaves $\est{ch19.main-green}{value}$ gates green on the
main copy, confirms the published verdicts wherever it can, and moves $\est{ch19.disagree}{value}$ red gate to not
assessable. Each red gate names the data the bank would have to collect to turn it green, and the report closes with
those requirements and the repairs the pipelines need.
