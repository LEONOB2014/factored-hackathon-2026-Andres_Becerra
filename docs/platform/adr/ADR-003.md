# ADR-003 · Postgres + pgvector as the operational and vector store

Status: accepted (2026-10) · revised 2026-10-05 · related [ADR-004](ADR-004.md), [ADR-008](ADR-008.md),
[ADR-021](ADR-021.md)

## Context
Products need low-latency lookups that the lakehouse is not built for:
- the copilot's card facts;
- a customer 360;
- online fraud features;
- the decision log;
- the governed knowledge base for retrieval.

These stores need transactions, least-privilege roles and audited access. The vector volume is small: tens of
policy documents chunked into dozens to thousands of passages. Separately, the audit trail must be immutable and
reachable only by audit roles.

## Decision drivers
- **Few technologies to secure and audit;** one managed service per region in the cloud.
- **Governance in the database:** only approved, in-window knowledge is visible to retrieval.
- **Least privilege per consumer** (publisher, reader, scorer, monitoring, lineage).
- **The audit store is isolated** from everything that serves products.

## Decision
1. **`pg-core`** (`pgvector/pgvector:pg16`) holds:
   - `bank_serving`: schemas `serving`, `online_features` and `decisions`, published from gold with digests;
   - `knowledge`: the governed knowledge-base registry plus `kb.chunk` vectors with an HNSW cosine index;
   - service databases for Airflow, MLflow, monitoring and Marquez.
2. **Retrieval reads only `kb.active_chunk`:** a view of approved, in-window versions, granted to `app_reader`. Draft,
   superseded, retired and expired versions are never visible.
3. **One role per consumer:**
   - `publisher` (publish_serving, kb_sync);
   - `app_reader` (read-only: services and agent tools);
   - `scorer` (append decisions, upsert online features);
   - `monitoring`;
   - `marquez`.
4. **`pg-audit`** is a separate Postgres instance on `net_audit` with **no published port**. It holds the hash-chained
   ledger, privacy budgets, subject keys, data-subject requests, compliance triggers and the GenAI audit tables.
5. **Cloud:** Cloud SQL Postgres per residency region (Terraform module `cloudsql_postgres`, not applied).

## Implementation
- `platform/docker/postgres-core/`:
  - `01_databases_and_roles.sh`;
  - `02_bank_serving.sql`;
  - `03_knowledge.sql` (the view, the HNSW index and the append-only triggers on the registry).
- `platform/docker/postgres-audit/sql/02_audit_schema.sql`.
- Publication: the `publish_serving` DAG; knowledge: `kb_sync` with `latam_platform/kb_pipeline.py`.

## Evidence and verification
- **7 serving tables published** with digests on the 2026-10-03 run (for example `customer_360` with 150,000 rows and
  `recent_transactions` with 4.28 M) ([platform README](../README.md)).
- **Knowledge base:** 9 approved documents active and reconciled across the registry, pgvector and Neo4j.
- **The governed view holds in practice:**
  - connected as `app_reader`, the copilot's pgvector retriever is refused on `kb.chunk` and reads
    `kb.active_chunk`;
  - its active set hashes identically to Neo4j and to the bundled index (`build_kb_index.py --verify` in
    `copilot/scripts/`).

## Consequences
- **Positive:**
  - one hardened technology for operational data and vectors;
  - governance enforced by grants and views, not by application code;
  - mature backup and residency options (Cloud SQL in region).
- **Negative:**
  - pgvector HNSW is comfortable up to millions of chunks, not billions;
  - vectors and operational rows share one instance's resources.
- **Risks:**
  - a misgranted role exposes raw chunks. Mitigated: retrieval roles are granted the view only, and drafts are
    rejected at sync.

## Alternatives considered
- **A dedicated vector database** (rejected): another store to secure, back up and audit, for no gain at this size.
  It would win at hundreds of millions of vectors or with heavy filtered ANN workloads.
- **Serving from the lakehouse directly** (rejected): DuckDB has a single writer, no row-level security and no
  concurrent low-latency reads.

## Revisit when
- **Chunks exceed about 10 M,** or retrieval latency at p95 exceeds its budget.
- **Real customer data arrives:** add row-level security on serving tables, keyed by country (ADR-007).
- **The copilot moves from its snapshot to live serving reads** (ADR-021 follow-up): Cloud SQL per region.
