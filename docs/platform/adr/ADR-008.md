# ADR-008 · Crypto-shredding to reconcile immutability and erasure

Status: accepted (2026-10)

## Context
Audit records must be immutable; data-protection laws grant erasure rights.

## Decision
Personal fields in immutable records are encrypted with per-subject keys; erasure destroys the key; derived operational stores are purged; legal holds block erasure.

## Consequences
Chains stay verifiable and erasure is provable. Key management becomes critical (KMS/HSM in production).

## Alternatives considered
Deleting audit rows (rejected: breaks immutability); never erasing (rejected: non-compliant).
