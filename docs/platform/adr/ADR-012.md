# ADR-012 · Evidence-gated model portfolio

Status: accepted (2026-10)

## Context
Seven candidate targets were evaluated out of time on the whole bank, on each country and on the backup dataset:
- point-in-time features, gradient boosting, AUC with intervals, AP against the base rate;
- a one-feature baseline;
- the leaking `fraud_score` as a positive control.

Results:
- Only dormancy in the next 90 days is learnable (AUC 0.73), and the six-month transaction count alone matches the
  model.
- Campaign conversion is weak to moderate (0.63–0.65).
- Fraud, complaint in 90 days, delinquency and contact escalation show no evidence of signal.

The same verdicts hold on the backup. Evidence: [strategy 12 §12.4](../../strategy/12_development_path.md).

## Decision
- **Rules first.** A target without demonstrated signal gets transparent rules, monitoring and the data collection
  that would make it learnable, written as an **entry criterion** (for example confirmed fraud labels with dates, or
  12 months of days-past-due history).
- **A model is built only when its target passes the protocol of `eda/notebooks/country_template/14`.** It must beat
  the one-feature benchmark by a margin outside the interval, or be preferred for transparency when it does not.
- **The first model is dormancy,** as a per-customer rate model with the one-feature rule as the benchmark.
- **Every evaluation includes the leak control**, and is deterministic: a stable row order and a fixed seed.

## Consequences
- No supervised fraud, complaint, delinquency or escalation model until its entry criterion is met. Their roadmap
  slots move to data collection.
- Model cards carry the benchmark and the control.
- Learnability probes (Kumo-style) run first on real data for every new target.

## Alternatives considered
- Train every planned model and let validation decide (rejected: it spends effort on targets the data cannot
  support, and invites leakage).
- Deep models first (rejected: no signal for boosting means none for deeper models on the same features).
