# ADR-007 · Residency strategy per country

Status: accepted (2026-10)

## Context
MX and BR have in-country GCP regions; CO and AR do not.

## Decision
Per-country environments (mx, br, latam-shared); org policy restricts resource locations; CO/AR proposed in southamerica-east1 under a documented transfer basis pending legal sign-off; only DP aggregates and approved model weights cross borders; federated learning keeps rows in silos.

## Consequences
Compliant by construction where regions exist; explicit legal dependency for CO/AR.

## Alternatives considered
Single region for all countries (rejected: residency).
