# ADR-019 · The readiness control plane is the product

Status: accepted (2026-10)

## Context
The exploratory phase found that the event-level data carries almost no behavioural signal:
- labels leak;
- joins are valid but wrong;
- every process is drawn independently.

Re-grained by country, day, month, campaign cell and hour, it carries forecastable aggregates, one learnable target
(dormancy) and a set of controls only a given grain can see. The most useful thing the bank can operate is not one
more model. It is the system that knows, per scope and grain:
- what the data can support today;
- why a model is not trainable yet;
- what must be collected for it to become trainable;
- which deterministic services can ship meanwhile.

The two atlases (`eda/reports/dashboards/grain_atlas/`) already present this view: grains × stages, gates, audit
items and a shared decision board.

## Decision
1. **The product is a readiness control plane over the lakehouse.** Its console grows from the Grain Atlas. For every
   scope (ADR-016) and grain (ADR-017) it shows:
   - data health: contracts, holds, completeness;
   - the readiness scorecard (ADR-018);
   - the data-collection audit with owners and acceptance tests;
   - the decision board.
2. **The model lifecycle runs through it as a loop:**
   1. a gate turns green, or a requirement lands;
   2. a model spec, plan and ADR are drafted from the audit row and the evidence ledger (ADR-015);
   3. training with hyperparameter search and experiment tracking (Optuna, MLflow) against the gate's benchmark;
   4. A/B or holdout test;
   5. deployment behind the gate;
   6. monitoring of drift, calibration and the KPI;
   7. back to the scorecard.
3. **Decision rights follow RAPID:**
   - **Recommend**: models and language agents;
   - **Agree**: deterministic policy and gates, which can veto;
   - **Perform**: the platform executes;
   - **Input**: the marts and the evidence ledger;
   - **Decide**: an accountable human for high-risk outcomes.

   Circuit breakers stop automation when a gate, a contract or a monitor fails.
4. **The customer-service agent is the first consumer.** It reads only trusted marts, acts through policy-checked
   tools, and hands off to a human with the verified facts. Its own learned components pass the same gate.

## Consequences
- Value is delivered before any model is trainable: deterministic services, controls and the audit itself.
- Each new data source changes the console automatically (re-run gates), which makes the case for data investment
  measurable.
- The atlas must move from a static page to an application with live reads of `agg.*`, the scorecard and the
  decision board; its current content is the specification of that application.

## Alternatives considered
- **A single end-user AI assistant as the whole product** (rejected as the product, kept as its first consumer): on
  this data it would rest on rules alone and hide why.
- **A generic MLOps platform** (rejected): without the readiness gates and the audit, it would train and deploy
  models the data cannot support.
