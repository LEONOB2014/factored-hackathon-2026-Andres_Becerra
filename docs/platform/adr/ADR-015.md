# ADR-015 · The exploratory record and what it decided

Status: accepted (2026-10)

## Context
The exploratory phase (v0.1.0 to v0.3.0) asked one question at a time of a synthetic LATAM retail bank:
- 13 CSV tables with no metadata;
- 23.5 M main records from 2023-06-17 to 2026-06-17, plus a 20.8 M-record "backup" folder;
- 150,000 customers in Mexico, Colombia and Argentina.

Each answer changed what the platform had to do. The work is spread over the notebook series in `eda/notebooks/`,
the tables in `eda/reports/tables/` and two atlases in `eda/reports/dashboards/grain_atlas/` (the Data Atlas and the
Grain Atlas). This record collects it in one place so later decisions can cite it.

## Decision
The table below is the evidence ledger of the exploratory phase. Each row is a question, where it was answered, the
number that settled it and what it decided. Later ADRs, specs and models cite a row instead of re-deriving it.

| # | Question | Where | Settled by | Decided |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is in the data, and what can each table be trusted for? | `medallion/01`, root `01` | 0.81 transactions per customer-month; R25: 66.4 % of complaints name another customer's product; R27: 100 % of transcripts are templates with unrendered slots; R21: 50.1 % of sends lack current consent | Population features and rate models only; semantic (ownership) foreign-key tests; transcripts used only to prototype the NLP pipeline |
| 2 | Can fraud be learned? | root `09`, `pipeline/09` | `is_fraud` is a function of `fraud_score`; behaviour only: AUC 0.513; point-in-time, out of time: 0.504 | No supervised fraud model on this label (ADR-012) |
| 3 | Is the backup folder a copy? | root `02`–`06` | Classifier two-sample AUC 0.989 on contacts; 0 identical products among 128,599 shared ids; about 7 % of people found in both by record linkage | A separate source with its own lineage, never history; a stability test set |
| 4 | What can be detected without labels? | root `07`–`11` | 1 % planted anomalies of 5 types; top-3 rank ensemble AP 0.773 against COPOD 0.687; dormant reactivation missed by every strong detector | Ensemble monitor plus a dedicated dormancy rule; planted-anomaly CI for detectors |
| 5 | Did the source ever change shape? | `model_risk/01` | 12,504 files, 0 unexplained lines; 10 of 10 planted schema changes detected on the day | Contract-driven silver with a drift circuit breaker |
| 6 | Can the source be kept exactly? | `pipeline/02` | 44.3 M records re-serialised hash-equal; 171.9 M cells where a typed loader turned empty into NULL | Lossless bronze (text plus sha256 per record) |
| 7 | Can data be corrected without losing the truth? | `model_risk/02` | 109 / 109 relations equal after revert and in the as-of rebuild | Four-eyes correction overlay with revert and as-of rebuilds |
| 8 | Is gold reproducible? | `pipeline/06`, `pipeline/12` | 25 / 108 relations differed between two builds; 0 / 108 after the fix | Decimal sums, ordered aggregates, stable ties; CI forbids the drifting constructs |
| 9 | Does the pipeline do what we think? | `pipeline/01`–`12` | Replay of the compiled DAG: 109 relations, 107 tests green, 6 silent defects (NULL token supernode, 19 % of fact keys NULL, stale eligibility, discarded dispute candidates, a dead AML typology, misnamed fraud columns) | The fix list; tests of business invariants, not only shape |
| 10 | Is it one bank or three? | `country_{mx,co,ar,all}`, `country_compare/01` | A blended contract holds every transaction day of each country (1,099, 1,099 and 1,098); own baselines hold 0; behaviour and learnability identical | Country-keyed configuration, one code base (ADR-011); cut at bronze (ADR-016) |

The atlases render every row with its charts, concept notes and decisions; they are the readable form of this record.

## Consequences
- **The phase's conclusion is stated once:** this data supports a trusted platform, deterministic services over its
  marts, forecastable aggregates and one rate model (dormancy). It does not support behavioural models. Every gap is
  owned as a data requirement (strategy chapter 13).
- **New work starts from a row, not from the raw data.** A spec cites the row whose evidence it relies on.
- The notebooks stay the executable evidence; this ADR is the index. A number changes here only when a notebook is
  re-executed.

## Alternatives considered
- **Leave the evidence in the notebooks and strategy chapters** (rejected): a dozen series and two atlases are not citable
  from a spec or an ADR without an index.
- **One ADR per notebook series** (rejected): the decisions cross series; the ledger shows each in one row.
