# LFPDPPP 2025 (summary)

## Status
A new Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties was published on 20 March 2025 and entered into force on 21 March 2025. Oversight moved from INAI to the Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance.

## Data implication
Privacy notices, consent and the handling of automated processing require review. Summary for internal use; verify with counsel.
