# Generative AI use policy

## Decisions
Language models explain and draft; they never compute amounts, approve credit or move money. Decisions come from rules and validated models.

## Grounding
Every number in an answer must come from a tool result or a retrieved document; ungrounded answers are blocked.

## Audit
Model version, exact system instructions, retrieved documents, redacted inputs and outputs, guardrail verdicts and human overrides are recorded in the audit store.
