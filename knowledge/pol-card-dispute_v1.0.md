# Card transaction dispute policy (v1.0, superseded)

## Scope
Applies to unrecognised card charges reported through any channel.

## Intake
The agent records the complaint and searches the customer's recent transactions to find the disputed one.

## Provisional credit
Provisional credit is evaluated case by case by the disputes team within 10 business days.
