# MED 2.0 for Pix (summary)

## What it is
MED 2.0 is the second version of the Pix special return mechanism of the Banco Central do Brasil. It adds an in-app dispute for suspected fraud and the tracing and blocking of funds across up to five subsequent layers of accounts.

## Since when
It became mandatory on 2 February 2026 under BCB Resolution 493/2025.

## Data implication
Tracing requires counterparty identifiers, Pix keys and end-to-end identifiers on every transfer. Summary for internal use; verify with counsel.
